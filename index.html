What's inside

Real ingredients with long histories.

Not clean, not natural, not wellness — intentional. Everything in KirkaDay is something you'd recognize from a kitchen, and most of it has been poured at somebody's table for centuries.

Hibiscus

The heart of it

The deep-red calyx of Hibiscus sabdariffa, the flower behind karkadeh, bissap, agua de jamaica and sorrel.

Tamarind

A sour pod with a devoted following

Poured as tamr hindi to break the fast from Cairo to Damascus, and as agua de tamarindo across Mexico.

Cardamom

The spice of welcome

Cracked open for guests across the Arabian Peninsula and simmered into chai across South Asia.

Cinnamon

Warmth from bark

Stirred into café de olla and horchata in Mexico, and dusted over sahlab across the Middle East.

In the glass: tart and floral up front, warm and spiced on the finish, lightly sparkling throughout.

Where I entered the story

It started at the iftar table.

Growing up, the drink was karkadeh — one of those flavors woven into life, especially Ramadan. Cold, deep red, tart and sweet, waiting at the table when it was finally time to break the fast. For years I thought of it simply as something from our part of the world.

Then I started noticing it elsewhere. In Mexico, agua de jamaica. Across West Africa, bissap. In the Caribbean, sorrel. Different names, different spices, different traditions — and again and again, people had made this extraordinary red flower part of their tables.

I didn't realize how much bigger its story was.

I love the craft of food — where an ingredient comes from, and why people have reached for it for generations. That's what felt missing when I looked at the modern beverage aisle.

  • I didn't want an energy drink that tasted like an invented color.
  • And I didn't want to bury a traditional drink under a mountain of sweetness either.

I wanted something I could reach for and think: I understand what I'm drinking. So we started with real hibiscus, then tamarind, cardamom and cinnamon — and kept working. Batches too tart. Too sweet. Too heavy. Too weak.

KirkaDay™ is a modern sparkling energy drink, made with respect for a flower people have loved for generations.
