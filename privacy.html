Privacy

Kirkaday LLC (“KirkaDay,” “we,” “us”) runs kirka.day. This page explains, in plain English, what we collect, why, and who helps us handle it.

The short version

  • We collect only what you give us: your email (and ZIP, if you share it) for the Sunday email, and business contact details if you fill in the wholesale form.
  • We don’t sell your data, and we don’t run analytics or ad pixels on this site right now.
  • You can unsubscribe anytime, or ask us to delete your information by writing to hello@kirka.day.

The email signup

When you join the list, your details go to Beehiiv, the service that sends our newsletter and stores the subscriber list for us. Beehiiv processes:

  • your email address;
  • your ZIP code, if you enter one;
  • which page you signed up from (the home page or the join page);
  • your answer to the optional “go-to drink” question on the thank-you page, if you give one;
  • campaign details from the link that brought you here, if any: UTM tags (source, medium, campaign, term, content) and the referring website.

Welcome email. You’re added as soon as you sign up, and Beehiiv sends you a welcome email right away.

Unsubscribe anytime. Every email has an unsubscribe link, or you can email us and we’ll remove you.

We use your ZIP only to see where interest is, so we can plan Houston tastings and first stores and tell you about ones near you. Past emails are published at log.kirka.day, which Beehiiv hosts under its own privacy policy.

The wholesale form

The “Stock KirkaDay” form on our wholesale page is run by Tally. It collects business contact details, such as your name, business name, email, phone, location, and whatever you tell us about your store or distribution. Submissions are stored in Google Sheets and emailed to our team. We use them only to reply to you about wholesale. Wholesale submissions are never added to the newsletter.

Hosting and logs

kirka.day is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel automatically processes standard request data, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time, to deliver the site, keep it running and protect it from abuse. Those logs are kept for a limited time under Vercel’s own policies. The site’s fonts load from Google Fonts, which also receives your IP address when your browser fetches them.

What we don’t do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels or advertising cookies on kirka.day right now. If that changes, we’ll update this page first.
  • No selling or renting of your personal information, and no sharing it for advertising.

Links and embeds

Links to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies. If we embed a YouTube video, it uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and loads only when it scrolls into view.

Your choices

Email hello@kirka.day to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, and we’ll take care of it. This site isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the effective date above. Questions? Kirkaday LLC, Houston, Texas · hello@kirka.day
